### About Me

Curiosity, problem-solving, and storytelling have shaped my path from working behind the scenes in film production to building AI and data-driven applications. Moving into data science and AI engineering was a natural extension of my interest in uncovering patterns and translating complex systems into reliable tools people can actually use.

Today, my work spans both **machine learning** and **end-to-end AI engineering**:
* **AI Systems & Agents:** Building agentic workflows, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines, and personal AI assistants, while leveraging AI coding agents across full-stack development.
* **Machine Learning & Data Science:** Training and evaluating models with Python (**Scikit-learn**, **TensorFlow**), exploring data with **pandas** and **seaborn**, and designing data pipelines—from natural disaster prediction models to K-Nearest Neighbors recommendation systems.
* **Backend & Deployment:** Structuring relational databases (**MySQL**, **SQLite**), building APIs with **FastAPI**, and containerizing applications with **Docker** for clean, scalable delivery.
